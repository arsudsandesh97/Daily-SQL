# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying At-Risk Loyalty Members with Declining Engagement

## Business Scenario
You are a Data Analyst at "LoyalMart," a popular e-commerce company that runs a premium loyalty program. LoyalMart's loyalty program offers exclusive benefits, discounts, and points that members can redeem for purchases. The marketing team is concerned about member retention and wants to proactively identify "at-risk" members whose engagement with the program (specifically, point redemptions) has significantly decreased recently compared to their historical activity. This initiative aims to re-engage these members before they churn or stop utilizing program benefits.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2024-03-15'
```

# Task
Identify active loyalty members who are "at-risk" due to a significant drop in their loyalty point redemption activity.

An active loyalty member is considered "at-risk" if:
1.  They have an 'Active' loyalty membership status.
2.  Their number of `recent_redemptions` (redemptions in the last 30 days from `CURRENT_DATE`) is less than 50% of their `historical_redemptions`.
3.  They had at least 2 `historical_redemptions` (redemptions in the 180 days prior to the `recent_redemptions` period). This ensures we only flag members with a meaningful history of engagement.
    *   **Recent Period:** `CURRENT_DATE - 30 days` to `CURRENT_DATE`.
    *   **Historical Period:** `CURRENT_DATE - 210 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 31 days`.

Return the `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `email`, `recent_redemptions`, `historical_redemptions`, and the `percentage_drop` (rounded to 2 decimal places). The `percentage_drop` should be calculated as `((historical_redemptions - recent_redemptions) * 100.0 / historical_redemptions)`.
