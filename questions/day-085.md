# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Cross-Category Shopper Identification for Targeted Marketing

## Business Scenario
A marketing analyst at "ShopSmart," a large e-commerce company, is tasked with identifying potential cross-sell opportunities. ShopSmart's data suggests that customers who frequently purchase from the 'Electronics' category might also be interested in products from the 'Home & Kitchen' category (e.g., smart home devices, kitchen gadgets). The analyst wants to find loyal 'Electronics' shoppers who have **never** purchased anything from 'Home & Kitchen' to target them with a special introductory offer for Home & Kitchen products. They also need to know the total amount these identified customers have spent specifically on 'Electronics' products.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-11-15'
```

```sql
CREATE TABLE Customers (
    customer_id INT PRIMARY KEY,
    customer_name VARCHAR(100),
    customer_email VARCHAR(100) UNIQUE
```

# Task
Identify customers who have purchased at least one item from the 'Electronics' category but have **never** purchased any item from the 'Home & Kitchen' category. For these customers, calculate their total spend on 'Electronics' products.
The output should list the customer's name, email, and their total spend on 'Electronics' products, ordered by customer name alphabetically.

---

# Expected Output Format
| customer_name | customer_email | total_electronics_spend |
|---------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Alice Smith   | alice@example.com | 2170.00                 |
| Emily Brown   | emily@example.com | 1195.00                 |
