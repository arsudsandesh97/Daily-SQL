# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Loyal Lapsed Shoppers Identification for Targeted Re-Engagement

## Business Scenario
A leading fashion retailer uses an analytics platform provided by a SaaS company. The marketing team at the retailer is planning a targeted re-engagement campaign for their most valuable customers. They want to identify "Loyal Lapsed Shoppers"—customers who meet specific criteria:
1.  They are premium subscribers.
2.  They are historically high-spenders (in the top 3 customers by total historical spend).
3.  They have specific "preferred" product categories (categories from which they've made at least 2 distinct purchases historically).
4.  Crucially, they have NOT made *any* purchases from *any* of their preferred product categories in the last 60 days.

Your task as a Data Analyst is to identify these "Loyal Lapsed Shoppers" to help the marketing team design personalized campaigns.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify the `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `total_historical_spend`, and a comma-separated list of their `preferred_categories` for all "Loyal Lapsed Shoppers".

A customer is considered a "Loyal Lapsed Shopper" if ALL of the following conditions are met:
1.  They have a `subscription_status` of 'Premium'.
2.  Their `total_historical_spend` ranks among the top 3 highest spenders across all customers.
3.  They have at least one "preferred" product category. A category is "preferred" for a customer if they have made at least 2 distinct purchases (orders) that included products from that category.
4.  They have NOT purchased *any* product from *any* of their preferred categories within the last 60 days from `CURRENT_DATE`.

Order the results by `total_historical_spend` in descending order.
