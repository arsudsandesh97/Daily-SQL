# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Customer Primary Category Preference Shift

## Business Scenario
An e-commerce company's marketing team wants to identify customers whose primary product category preference has shifted significantly between two consecutive 90-day periods. Understanding these shifts can help in targeted marketing campaigns, personalized recommendations, and even inventory planning. You are a Data Analyst tasked with identifying such customers.

A customer's "primary product category" for a given period is defined as the category from which they purchased the *most unique products* during that period. In case of a tie in the number of unique products, the category with the *lower `category_id`* should be chosen as the primary.

The analysis should focus on customers who made at least one purchase in *both* the current 90-day period and the previous 90-day period.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Identify customers who have changed their primary product category between the "previous 90-day period" and the "current 90-day period".

The "current 90-day period" is from `CURRENT_DATE - 89 days` to `CURRENT_DATE`.
The "previous 90-day period" is from `CURRENT_DATE - 179 days` to `CURRENT_DATE - 90 days`.

For each such customer, return their `customer_id`, `customer_name`, their `previous_primary_category_name`, and their `current_primary_category_name`.

Only include customers who made at least one purchase in *both* periods.
