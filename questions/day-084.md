# SQL Scenario-Based Interview Question: Identifying Dormant High-Value Customers for Retention

## Business Scenario
A leading e-commerce company, which sells a variety of products and subscription boxes, is focused on improving customer retention. The marketing team wants to launch a highly targeted re-engagement campaign for customers who were historically valuable but have become inactive recently. As a Data Analyst, your task is to identify these "dormant high-value customers" based on specific business rules.

Assume:
```sql
CURRENT_DATE = '2023-10-26'
```

# Task
Write a SQL query to identify "dormant high-value customers" based on the following criteria:

1.  **Registered long enough**: The customer registered at least 365 days ago (from `CURRENT_DATE`).
2.  **Historically high-value**: The customer made at least 3 purchases within their first 90 days following their `registration_date`.
3.  **Currently dormant**: The customer has made **no** purchases in the last 180 days (from `CURRENT_DATE`).

Your query should return the `customer_id`, `email`, `registration_date`, and the `total_first_90_days_orders` (the count of orders meeting criteria #2) for these customers.
